# Licensing decision — open for future product releases

The migrated prototype components already carry MIT licenses. Their notices remain
unchanged in apps/website and packages/discovery, including the baseline. This migration
does not revoke or silently relicense them. New project documentation/checklists are
provided under the accompanying MIT license.

Before public release, Daniel should explicitly decide whether future original work
continues as MIT, uses another open-source license, or introduces separately licensed
paid tooling/support. Review component provenance and dependencies first. Existing
MIT grants remain applicable to existing covered code. No revenue or pricing claim,
legal clearance or approval of a new commercial license is implied.
