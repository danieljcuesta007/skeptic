THE IDEA

Your knowledge.
One lasting home.

Skeptic is being built to help different AI tools work from the same notes, preferences and project checkpoints. Your knowledge stays in your Markdown folder or Obsidian vault. You choose the model.

WHY SKEPTIC

Less repetition. More continuity.

Keep ownership.

Readable files you control, in the tools you already use.

Repeat yourself less.

Shared preferences help the next AI understand how you work.

Keep moving.

Clear checkpoints preserve progress when you switch models.

HOW IT WORKS / THE PLANNED EXPERIENCE

Three simple steps.

01

Start with your files.

Keep your notes where they are. Markdown and Obsidian are the starting formats.

02

Choose your boundaries.

Decide what the AI may read. Reading never grants permission to edit or publish.

03

Carry progress forward.

Use a project checkpoint to show what’s done and what comes next.
