THE IDEA

Your knowledge.
One lasting home.

Skeptic is not another AI. It is the independent continuity layer for supported AI agents. Own your knowledge, connect your AI tools, switch with context, and return caught up. Claude → Codex → Claude: the returning agent receives the latest accessible context, with verified Git evidence separated from documented claims. Obsidian is preferred, not required; ordinary Markdown works too. Hidden session state cannot be recovered.

WHY SKEPTIC

Less repetition. More continuity.

Keep ownership.

Readable files you control, in the tools you already use.

Repeat yourself less.

Shared preferences help the next AI understand how you work.

Keep moving.

Clear checkpoints preserve progress when you switch models.
