02 / How it works

Three steps. One command.

  1. 01

    Checkpoint

    Skeptic reads the session you pick: your last request, the files it changed, and the project's own notes. Only folders you approved, nothing else.

  2. 02

    Choose

    Pick the window and the engine: Codex, Hermes, or Claude again once it's back. Every open session is listed separately, so the ones you're saving stay put.

  3. 03

    Continue

    The new engine gets the handoff and carries on in the same folder. Your original Claude session stays intact, so you can go back to it.
